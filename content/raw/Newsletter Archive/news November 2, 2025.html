📉 Bank of Canada Lowers Key Interest Rate by 0.25% – What It Means for You

📉 Bank of Canada Lowers Key Interest Rate by 0.25% – What It Means for You
By Keith Uthe – Enrich Mortgage Group

 

The Bank of Canada has announced a 0.25% decrease to their key interest rate, bringing some welcomed relief to borrowers. For most lenders, this means the Prime lending rate now sits at 4.45%.

 

However, the Bank has also made it clear — they’re pressing pause on further rate cuts for now.

 

What does this mean for you?

 

If you have a variable-rate mortgage, line of credit, or adjustable-rate product, you’ll likely see a small reduction in your payments. For buyers and investors, this could be a signal to re-evaluate opportunities before rates shift again.

 

As always, understanding how these changes affect your personal mortgage strategy is key.
