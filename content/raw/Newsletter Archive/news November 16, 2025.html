Should You Renew or Refinance Your Mortgage in 2026? Consider these for your Mortgage
Renewal

 

By Keith Uthe, Mortgage Broker | Enrich Mortgage Group

 

As 2026 approaches, many Canadians are asking: “What are the strategies, options and opportunities available to me at the time of my mortgage renewal—and could it transform my financial future?” The answer is yes—but transformation doesn’t come from chasing the lowest rate. It comes from aligning your mortgage with your life’s deeper intentions.

 

From Rate Shopping to Purpose-Driven Planning
Most homeowners focus on what they want—“a lower rate” or “faster payoff.” But the real question is why. Freedom? Flexibility? Peace of mind? When your “why” is clear, every financial decision gains direction and purpose. Your mortgage is likely the largest financial tool you’ll ever use. When structured with intention, it can reduce stress and transform how you build wealth.


Why 2026 Is a Pivotal Year?
Thousands of Canadians will renew mortgages in 2026 amid changing rates and stricter lending criteria. This is your opportunity to pause and ask:
• Does my mortgage still reflect my life. my budget and goals?
• Am I paying for flexibility I’m not using—or missing flexibility I need?
• Could I use this renewal to improve cash flow, consolidate debt, or start investing using the Smith Manoeuvre?
As I often tell clients, “Your mortgage should be part of building wealth by design—not by accident.”
