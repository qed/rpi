I hope you had a wonderful Thanksgiving Monday, surrounded by family, friends, and maybe even a little too much pumpkin pie! 🍁 It’s such a special time to pause, reflect, and give thanks for all the good things — and for us at RPI Education, there’s been a lot to be grateful for lately.

The proposed changes aim to allow more unit builds and conversions of single-family homes, which could create a whole new wave of housing options and investment potential in our markets. It’s always energizing to see progress in action!

 

Then came Wednesday, which was nothing short of inspiring. We had an incredible time at BlZCON, followed by the Real Estate Investors Summit, where I had the privilege of sharing the stage with Drew Toth of Elevate Living, the team from Cardinal Law, the experts at Stonegate Legal Services, and Kevin Bretner of CRESI. Keith Uthe dove deep into the new OFSI regulations, helping us all understand the fine print and how to strategically navigate them as investors. It was an inspiring session packed with value, insight, and connection — just the way we like it! The video's available for members to view on the private Facebook Members Forum.
