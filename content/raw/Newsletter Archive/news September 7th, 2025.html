Home sales in August showed continued signs of recovery from July as inventory levels declined. However, supply remains well above historical averages and as expected, average sale prices fell across all property types. While the modest rebound in sales is encouraging for sellers, the market still carries a significant volume of inventory, keeping conditions favourable for buyers.

“The August sales figures add further confirmation that sales activity across Metro Vancouver appears to be recovering, albeit somewhat slowly, from the challenging first half of the year,” said Andrew Lis, GVR’s director of economics and data analytics, "Prices have eased around two per cent since the start of the year and are down about one percent month over month in August, signalling that sellers have been willing to lower price expectations... As sellers’ and buyers’ expectations have become more aligned, transaction volume has picked up.” 

As noted in last month's update, these buyer-friendly conditions—driven by elevated supply—may begin to shift if inventory continues to tighten, potentially restoring some bargaining power to sellers, though this may not occur until closer to the end of the year. The next Bank of Canada rate announcement is scheduled for September 17th. Should interest rates be reduced, we’ll have a clearer sense of where the market is headed as we approach year-end and look toward early 2026.

Here's the summary for August:

2.9% ↑ in sales since August 2024 (demand)
19.2% ↓ below the 10-year August sales average
17.6% ↑ in listed properties compared to August 2024 (supply)
36.9% ↑ above the 10-year August listings average

The MLS® Home Price Index composite benchmark price for all residential properties in Metro Vancouver is currently $1,150,400. This represents a 3.5% decrease from August 2024 and a 1.3% decrease compared to July 2025.
