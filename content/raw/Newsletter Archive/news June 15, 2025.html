Toronto Real Estate Is Quiet… But Returns Are Hitting 17% (That’s Before Appreciation!)

 

Let’s cut to it. The Toronto real estate market is quiet with a ton of listings— but the smart investors aren’t sitting still.

 

Here’s what we’re seeing right now:

 

✔️ Prices are still low (down 20% from peak)

✔️ Interest rates are lower and still coming down

✔️ Cash flows are stronger than we’ve seen in years

 

We’re talking 11% cash-on-cash returns from Toronto multiplexes — no renovation risk, no major delays — just rented day one. And once you add principal paydown? You’re seeing 17%+ income returns on your capital.

 

That’s before factoring in appreciation or value-add upside.
 
 
 
 
 
 

Isn’t the Toronto Real Estate Market Still Falling?

Not exactly. It’s soft, yes — but stable. Here’s what’s actually happening:

 

1. Supply is tight for low-rise homes.

We’re not building enough family housing. Condos? Overbuilt. But actual houses in Toronto? Supply is super limited. You literally have to tear one down to build another.

 

2. Sellers aren’t desperate.

No flood of distressed listings. Most sellers aren’t panicking. Interest rates around 4% are manageable, and lenders are helping owners stay afloat.

 

3. Build costs are high = price floor.

Labour and material costs haven’t dropped much. That puts a bottom under house values.
 
 
 

4. The big correction already happened.

Prices already dropped 20% — and in strong end-user areas, they’ve been flat for almost 3 years.

 

5. Rate cuts are here.

We’ve already seen a year of rate cuts, and two more are expected by year-end. That improves affordability and confidence.
 
 
 
 

Policy Changes That Open More Doors
 

Just yesterday, Toronto’s Planning & Housing Committee gave the green light to the biggest low-rise housing update we’ve seen in years.

 

Here’s what’s about to change (City Council vote June 27):

  • ✅ Sixplexes allowed citywide (plus a garden suite)

  • ✅ Fourplexes allowed on each side of a semi (8 units total — no apartment designation)

  • ✅ No more staged projects for fourplexes + backyard suites to avoid multiple development charges

     

These new rules remove red tape, save you hundreds of thousands in upfront fees, and unlock major value potential across the city.
