Let’s build your 2026 mortgage strategy together
 

After a sluggish second quarter, Canada’s economy bounced back with 2.6% growth in the third quarter, helping the country avoid a recession—typically defined as when the economy shrinks for two quarters in a row. While this strong headline number is encouraging, much of the boost came from temporary factors like government spending and reduced imports rather than increased consumer or business activity.

 

That said, the overall momentum is helping stabilize financial markets, especially in housing. According to the Canadian Real Estate Association (CREA), national home sales ticked up in October, suggesting early signs of renewed market confidence.

 

While this rebound in the economy decreases the likelihood of a Bank of Canada drop in December, both fixed and variable mortgage rates have been showing signs of relief. If this trend continues, Canadians looking to renew or purchase in early 2026 may benefit from more favourable options than we saw earlier this year.

 

What this means for you

Every financial situation is different. Headlines about the economy, interest rates, and government housing programs do not tell the full story of what’s possible for your specific mortgage goals.

 

Whether you are buying your first home, making a move, or renewing your mortgage, the right advice can make all the difference.

As your mortgage professional, I can:

  • Compare options across dozens of lenders to find the right fit for your needs
  • Walk you through the pros and cons of fixed and variable rates
  • Help you secure a rate hold while you explore your next steps
  • Identify potential opportunities to improve cash flow
  • Ensure your mortgage continues to support your evolving financial goals, especially as the market shifts
