New Bare Trust Rules for December 31, 2026 Year Ends

The application of the new trust reporting rules to bare trusts, which have been postponed three times, are now set to be effective as of December 31, 2026 with the T3 trust return being due by March 31, 2027.
 
If you are the only person on title for a real estate property but in fact another person has a beneficial interest in the property, that is a bare trust which will have to be reported.
 
You may want to consider adding the other person to the title this month (December 2025), as this will no longer be a bare trust and will not be subject to reporting. This aligns legal and beneficial ownership, eliminating the bare trust entirely since both spouses would then hold both legal title and beneficial interests-no trustee/beneficiary separation exists.
 
Note: If you wait until any time in 2026 to do this, you will have to file the trust return for that year and there likely will be legal and accounting fees to accomplish this.
