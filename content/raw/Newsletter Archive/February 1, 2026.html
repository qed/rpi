Welcome to our charming waterfront rustic log cabin in beautiful Trent Lakes, a peaceful four-season escape designed for comfort, space, and classic cottage character. The moment you walk in, you'll be greeted by the warm scent of cedar, a true sign you've arrived at the lake. Set on a quiet and serene stretch of Buckhorn Lake, this cozy cottage offers the perfect balance of relaxation and fun for all ages.

Enjoy a small private beach with shallow water entry, ideal for kids to swim and play. Spend your days kayaking or paddle boarding, then gather in the evenings around the campfire (firewood included). If you are bringing a boat or planning to rent one, a boat launch is conveniently located right next door.

This four-season cottage is equipped with heating and air conditioning, making it comfortable year-round, including warm summer days and cooler shoulder seasons.

Inside, the open-concept main level features a fully stocked kitchen, dining area, and a cozy living space complete with an electric fireplace and TV, perfect for relaxing after a day on the water. The bonus basement is a guest favourite and offers another electric fireplace, TV, a 9-foot pool table, and plenty of games and puzzles for rainy days or cozy nights in.

Step outside to enjoy your morning coffee on the deck, cook meals on the BBQ, dine at the picnic table, or enjoy lawn games with family and friends.
