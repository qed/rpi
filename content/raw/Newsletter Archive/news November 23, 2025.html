This simple calculation saved my investor client from a $200k mistake

Quick story: A client of mine was new to investing and found a 10-unit building that "looked perfect."

The seller's marketing package claimed a $100,000 NOI and a market value of $2,000,000.
 
 

He plugged those numbers into the calculator.

  • NOI: $100,000
  • Purchase Price: $2,000,000
  • Result: 5.0% Cap Rate

Looked good, right?
   
 
 

Then, we did our own due diligence (like we discussed in the last two emails).

We found the real NOI was closer to $85,000 after accounting for proper vacancy, reserves, and management.

We also knew that properties in that area were actually trading closer to a 6.0% Cap Rate, not 5%.

So, we plugged our numbers into the "Property #2" side of the calculator.

  • NOI: $85,000
  • Assumed Cap Rate: 6.0%
  • Result: $1,416,667 Estimated Value

The property was overpriced by more than $580,000.

This simple 5-minute analysis saved him from a massive mistake. That's the power of running your own numbers.

Hope this helps.
