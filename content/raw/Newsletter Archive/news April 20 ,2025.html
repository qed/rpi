  Porte Communities Heartland Townhomes (cash-flowing) 320 Heartland Cres, Cochrane, AB

Newly Constructed Townhouse Project: 
The property is a new (2022 construction) 48-unit townhouse project located in the developing Heartland community of Cochrane, Alberta. The townhomes are arranged in nine clusters, each containing either four or eight units.

Two-Bedroom Units: All units are two-bedroom townhomes, featuring either 1.5 or 2.5 bathrooms. The property also includes 96 surface parking stalls.

 Ways this Pays: 

 The property value at exit is estimated to be approximately $17M, representing a gain of about $2.2M from the acquisition cost of $14.8M. 

 Property Size and Valuation: The property covers approximately 2.57 acres. An appraisal conducted by Altus Group Limited on April 22, 2023, valued the property "as complete" at $14,900,000.

 Purchase and Financing Details: The property was purchased on September 28, 2023, for $14,250,000. Financing was arranged through CMLS Financial Ltd., with a mortgage principal of $12,508,795 at an interest rate of 4.00%. The mortgage matures on December 1, 2028.

 The Plan

Porte Communities plans to hold the property for approximately 7 years as an income-producing asset, paying regular distributions to investors. Then, the it is estimated that investors will be exited by sale or refinance depending on market conditions.
