Toronto Off-Market Opportunity: 8-10+ Unit Development Site 

Unique off-market development opportunity - excellent missing middle project!

 

With the recent near citywide zoning change to allow sixplex's w/ NO Development Charges, combined with this project's unique OLT approval, there are a few profitable exit strategies here. 

 

This property is one of only a handful of lots in Toronto that won an OLT case, rezoning it to follow the Major Streets by-law (currently under appeal otherwise) today, allowing up to 6 storeys and 60 units as of right. That makes this one of the most permissively zoned lots in the city.

 

Key Highlights:

  • Location: 25’ x 110’ Major Street lot, steps from Eglinton West Subway (direct access to Union & Pearson).
  • Zoning: OLT-approved for 6 storeys / 60 units; as-of-right density with room to reimagine.
  • Current Proposed Plan: 3-storey / 10-unit apartment building. Avoids site plan approval, elevators, stricter fire requirements, etc. Strong 3-bed/2-bath mix plus affordable studios for CMHC MLI Select.
  • Alternative Build Path: 6+2 option (sixplex with two legal basement units). Development charges waived on 6 units, strong financing advantages, and future expansion potential.
  • Theoretically, this site could fit as many as 24-28 smaller units over a 6 storey build. 
  • Multiple CMHC MLI compliant build paths w/ big value lift no completion. 
  • Seller is asking $1.1M, with some flexibility available for a quick closing. 
  • Motivated seller, also willing to consider a VTB for the right offer.
