Maple Leaf Funds Super Flow-Through

Featuring Jackson Stafford

 

Discover how Maple Leaf’s 2025 Super Flow-Through allows investors to deduct 100% of their contribution from taxable income, access the new 30% Critical Mineral Exploration Tax Credit (CMETC), and invest directly in a diversified basket of Canadian companies that support domestic resource development. Together, these benefits can generate potential tax savings of up to 141% — helping investors maximize tax efficiency, support Canada’s critical mineral sector, and transform tax obligations into strategic investments.

The Bank of Canada announced today that it is cutting the overnight lending rate by 0.25% to 2.50%. The Bank Rate is now 2.75% and the deposit rate is 2.45%.
 
Here’s what that means:

    •    Variable-rate mortgages and lines of credit will see borrowing costs come down.
    •    Fixed-rate mortgages may also be influenced, depending on bond yields.
    •    The Bank noted that Canada’s economy has slowed, with weaker GDP growth and easing inflation, prompting this rate cut.
 
Market outlook:

Lower borrowing costs could give buyers more room in their budgets, which may spark new activity this fall. At the same time, sellers may benefit from renewed demand as affordability improves. If you’ve been thinking about making a move, the next few months could be an interesting window.
