What’s Changing at RPl Education in 2026

As we wrap up the year, we wanted to share a quick and friendly update from RPl Education about how our community, events, and education will look moving into the new year.

A Quick Year-in-Review

 

This year called for a bit of a reset. As markets shifted and investment decisions became more nuanced, we found that the most valuable conversations were happening in more focused, educational settings — where people could ask thoughtful questions, learn from experienced practitioners, and connect with others navigating similar decisions.

With that in mind, RPl Education has refined its focus to support deeper learning, better decision-making, and a more engaged community, while continuing to offer private advisory support where appropriate.

Events & Community Gatherings

 

We’ll be pausing most large public events and open programs, but the community itself isn’t going anywhere.

 

What continues (for members):

  • Private networking and community events

  • Private equity and passive investment education sessions

  • In-person real estate investment field training

Our focus is now on learning, connection, and meaningful participation amongst RPl Members to create a strong and cohesive membership community.

Advisory Roundtables & Education

 

Open group coaching programs will be paused.

 

Instead, we’ll be hosting a members-only monthly learning roundtable, led by RPl Education experts and guest specialists. Each month will focus on a specific real estate or private markets education topic, with plenty of time for questions, discussion, and networking.

 

What to expect:

  • Monthly expert-led learning sessions

  • Live Q&A so members can ask questions directly

  • Opportunities to connect with RPl experts and other professionals

  • Live deals and investment opportunities 

These sessions are designed to be educational, practical, and interactive — combining expert insight with real-world discussion, with more educational structure.  

Membership, Courses & Access

Here’s how access will work going forward:

  • Invitations to private market discussions, networking events, and in-person experiences are members-only

  • All courses remain available in the online store and will be displayed ln our weekly newsletter

  • There will be no promotions or discounts (less emails to your inbox!) 

  • Bootcamps and investor field training tours are exclusive to members
