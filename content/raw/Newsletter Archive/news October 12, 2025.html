Toronto’s market isn’t hot right now — and that’s exactly why this moment matters.
 
You’ll notice a pattern that repeats almost every year: prices flatten in the fall, buyers retreat for the rest of the year, then optimism builds early in the new year and prices rebound.
 
Now, we’re heading into that setup again — with one more rate cut still expected by year-end, leverage is squarely on the buyers’ side.
 
The sales-to-new-listings ratio has dropped to 28%, meaning only about one in every three to four listings is selling right now. 
 
That’s the softest the market’s been in years. Sellers are anxious, inventory’s high, and buyers with financing in place are calling the shots.
