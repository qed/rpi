**1. Executive Summary**

  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  *RPI Helper is an AI-powered conversion engine that transforms RPI Education's broken digital front door into an intelligent, always-on sales and onboarding system --- enabling Monika Jazyk to scale from 100 paying members toward her mission of educating one million people about financial literacy.*
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 The Problem
---------------

RPI Education is a real estate investment education company led by
Monika Jazyk that helps everyday people invest like the top 2%. Monika
is a recognized thought leader who generates significant awareness
through speaking engagements, podcast appearances, and LinkedIn.
However, the business has a critical conversion gap:

-   **The Members Portal link on the website is dead.** Interested
    prospects have no working self-serve path to become paying members.

-   **The website is outdated,** showing a global chapter network that
    no longer operates, creating a false impression of the current
    business.

-   **There is no sales team.** RPI previously had a sales team to
    convert leads; that team no longer exists.

-   **The nurture-to-conversion cycle takes up to 5 years** ---
    prospects hear Monika speak, join the 2,000-person Keap email list,
    and may take years to convert because there is no active conversion
    mechanism.

-   **The storefront is inactive.** Lower-price-point products (books,
    self-serve courses) exist or are planned but have no traffic or
    conversion funnel.

The result: Monika is generating demand she cannot capture. Every
podcast appearance, every speaking engagement, every LinkedIn post
drives people to a digital experience that fails to convert them.

1.2 The Solution
----------------

RPI Helper is an AI-powered system that replaces the broken front door
with an intelligent, modern conversion engine. It combines a redesigned
web presence with an AI assistant trained on Monika's extensive content
library to create a 24/7 digital version of the RPI sales and onboarding
experience. The system guides visitors from initial interest to purchase
without requiring Monika's direct involvement.

1.3 The Opportunity
-------------------

  ---------------------------------------------------- -----------------------------------------
  **Metric**                                           **Detail**
  Current paying members                               100 across 4 tiers
  Email list (Keap)                                    2,000 contacts, 36% open rate
  Revenue if 5% of list converts to Wealth Immersion   100 new members × \$1,997 = \~\$200K/yr
  Revenue if 1% converts to Personal Coaching          20 new members × \$8,000 = \~\$160K/yr

  ---------------------------------------------------- -----------------------------------------

Every future podcast appearance, speaking engagement, and LinkedIn post

upside.

2. Who It's For
===============

RPI Helper serves multiple user types, each with different needs and
conversion paths.

2.1 Primary Users
-----------------

**Warm Prospects (from Monika's Personal Brand)**

People who have heard Monika speak at an event, on a podcast, or found
her on LinkedIn. They are interested but don't know what to do next.
Today, they hit a broken website and leave. RPI Helper catches them,
engages them, and guides them to the right product.

**Newsletter Subscribers (2,000 in Keap)**

The existing email list with a 36% open rate. These are warm leads who
have been in the nurture cycle for months or years. RPI Helper gives
them a reason to click through from the newsletter and a clear path to
purchase when they do.

**Self-Directed Learners**

People searching for real estate investment education online. They want
to browse, learn, and buy at their own pace without booking a call. RPI
Helper's storefront and AI assistant serve this audience 24/7.

2.2 Secondary Users
-------------------

**Monika Jazyk (Business Owner)**

RPI Helper reduces Monika's time spent on initial discovery calls by
handling qualification and basic education automatically. It provides
her with analytics on what visitors are asking, what products interest
them, and where they drop off --- intelligence she doesn't have today.

**Existing Members**

Current members at any tier benefit from the AI assistant as a
supplementary resource --- a way to revisit concepts, ask follow-up
questions between coaching sessions, and access the "coach in your
pocket" experience at scale.

3.2 Detailed Feature Specifications
-----------------------------------

**3.2.1 Modern Landing Hub**

A completely redesigned web experience that accurately represents RPI
Education as it exists today. This is not a patch on the existing
website --- it is a new, standalone web application that can either
replace the current site or live alongside it.

**Core Requirements:**

-   **Hero section** with Monika's value proposition: helping everyday
    people invest like the top 2%. Clear, modern design. Video embed
    capability for Monika's intro content.

-   **About section** that accurately reflects RPI Education today ---
    Monika's credentials, the team of experts (wealth advisors, agents,
    brokers, lawyers, accountants), and the global reach of the
    education platform.

-   **Testimonials section** featuring real member success stories
    (content already exists on current site: Allen Rothwell, Santos
    Garcia, Huw Evans, Isaac Douven, etc.).

-   **Membership comparison** displaying all four tiers with clear
    pricing, included features, and direct "Join Now" buttons linking to
    Teachable checkout.

-   **Mobile-responsive design** --- a significant portion of traffic
    from LinkedIn and podcasts will come from mobile devices.

-   **SEO-optimized** with proper meta tags, structured data, and fast
    load times to capture organic search traffic over time.

**What Gets Removed:**

All references to the inactive chapter network, dead Members Portal
link, and outdated blog posts from 2023. The site should reflect the
real, current business --- not a ghost of what it used to be.

**3.2.2 AI Advisor ("Digital Monika")**

The centerpiece of RPI Helper. An AI-powered conversational assistant
that is trained on Monika's extensive content library and can engage
visitors the way Monika would on a discovery call --- with warmth,
expertise, and a genuine desire to help people build wealth.

**Training Data Sources:**

-   **Wealth Immersion program:** All 7 modules --- video transcripts,
    text content, downloadable materials, coaching tools

-   **Podcast appearances:** Transcripts from Monika's numerous podcast
    interviews (SA565, Passive Income, Multifamily AP360, Capital Stack,
    Ice Cream with Investors, and many more)

-   **Blog archive:** All existing blog posts on real estate investing,
    wealth building, and financial literacy

-   **Newsletter archive:** Weekly newsletter content from Keap

-   **Video content:** Transcripts from educational videos, speaker
    series recordings, and coaching call frameworks

-   **PDFs and downloads:** All existing educational materials and
    frameworks

**Core Capabilities:**

-   **Real estate investing education:** Answers questions about buying
    rental properties, creative financing, market analysis, property
    management, passive investments, JVs, and more --- using Monika's
    actual frameworks and language, not generic advice

-   **Financial literacy coaching:** Discusses mindset, taxes,
    incorporating, estate planning, insurance --- the full Wealth
    Immersion curriculum topics

-   **Needs assessment:** Asks questions to understand where the visitor
    is in their investing journey (beginner, experienced, looking for
    passive vs. active) and tailors responses accordingly

-   **Product recommendation:** Based on the conversation, recommends
    the most appropriate RPI membership tier or product with a clear
    explanation of why

-   **Warm handoff to Monika:** For prospects who are clearly ready for
    Personal Coaching or Elite, the AI suggests booking a discovery call
    and provides the booking link

**Tone and Personality:**

The AI Advisor must feel like a conversation with a knowledgeable,
encouraging mentor --- not a chatbot. It should reflect Monika's
communication style: warm, empowering, direct, and focused on taking
action. It should use phrases and frameworks that Monika actually uses.
It should never feel salesy; it should feel like genuine guidance from
someone who cares about your financial future.

**Guardrails:**

-   **Never provides specific investment advice** (e.g., "buy this
    property" or "invest in this fund"). Provides education and
    frameworks only.

-   **Always disclaims** that it is an AI educational assistant, not a
    licensed financial advisor.

-   **Stays on-topic:** real estate investing, wealth building,
    financial literacy, and RPI Education's offerings. Gracefully
    redirects off-topic questions.

-   **Escalation path:** If a question exceeds the AI's scope, it
    recommends booking a call with Monika or the RPI team.

**3.2.3 Self-Serve Onboarding Flow**

A structured, guided experience that replaces the discovery call for
prospects who are ready to buy but don't need (or want) a 1-on-1
conversation. This can be triggered from the AI Advisor conversation or
accessed directly from the landing page.

**The Flow:**

1.  **Step 1 --- Where are you now?** Quick self-assessment: investing
    experience level, current portfolio (if any), financial goals,
    timeline.

2.  **Step 2 --- What do you want?** Investment style preference:
    hands-on (buy and manage properties), hands-off (passive
    investments), education-first (learn before acting), or business
    building (for entrepreneurs).

3.  **Step 3 --- Here's what we recommend:** Based on answers, the
    system recommends a specific tier or product with a personalized
    explanation of why it's the right fit. Includes pricing, key
    benefits, and a prominent "Join Now" button.

4.  **Step 4 --- Optional: Book a call.** For those who want to talk to
    someone first, a direct booking link to Monika's calendar.

Every interaction in this flow captures the visitor's email and
responses for CRM integration, even if they don't purchase immediately.

**3.2.4 Storefront & Product Catalog**

A clean, browsable product showcase that makes it easy for visitors to
see everything RPI Education offers and purchase directly.

**Product Categories:**

  -------------------- ------------------------------------------ ------------------ -------------------------------
  **Category**         **Examples**                               **Price Range**    **Purchase Flow**
  Digital Products     E-books, guides, worksheets, analyzers     \$10--\$97         Direct download after payment
  Self-Serve Courses   Individual modules, topic courses          \$97--\$497        Teachable enrollment
  Memberships          4 tiers from Education & Events to Elite   \$50--\$2,000/mo   Teachable enrollment
  Events               Webinars, workshops, masterminds           Varies             Registration + payment
  -------------------- ------------------------------------------ ------------------ -------------------------------

The storefront should feature clear product cards with descriptions,
pricing, social proof (testimonial snippets), and a single prominent
call-to-action button per product. Each product links directly to its
Teachable checkout page or payment processor.

**3.2.5 Lead Intelligence Layer**

Every visitor interaction generates data. The Lead Intelligence Layer
captures, structures, and acts on that data.

**Data Captured:**

-   **Contact info:** Email address (required to continue AI
    conversation after initial exchange), name, location

-   **Conversation history:** What questions they asked the AI Advisor,
    what topics they explored

-   **Assessment results:** Answers from the self-serve onboarding flow

-   **Engagement signals:** Pages visited, products viewed, time on
    site, return visits

-   **Lead score:** An automatically calculated score based on
    engagement depth, readiness signals, and fit

**CRM Integration (Keap):**

-   **New contacts created automatically** with all captured data as
    custom fields

-   **Existing contacts enriched** with new interaction data appended to
    their record

-   **Tag-based segmentation:** Automatically tag contacts based on
    interests (e.g., "passive investing", "beginner", "high intent") for
    targeted email campaigns

-   **Hot lead alerts:** Notify Monika when a high-score lead completes
    the assessment or spends significant time with the AI Advisor

**Analytics Dashboard:**

A simple admin view showing key metrics: total visitors, AI
conversations started, assessments completed, products purchased, lead
scores, and top questions asked. This gives Monika intelligence she has
never had about what her audience wants and where they get stuck.

4. Build Plan
=============

  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  *This build plan is designed for a team of 4 elite AI-native engineers working in an intensive hackathon format. The plan is structured in priority-ordered phases. The core challenge: get as deep into these phases as possible during the 30-hour weekend, then continue building in the weeks that follow. Each phase delivers standalone value --- the team ships something usable at every checkpoint.*
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Phase 1: Foundation (Hours 0--8)
--------------------------------

**Goal:** A live, modern landing page with working product links that
can immediately replace the broken website.

**Deliverables**

-   **Modern single-page application** built in Next.js/React with
    responsive design

-   **Hero section** with Monika's core message, video embed slot, and
    primary CTA

-   **Membership comparison section** with all 4 tiers, pricing,
    features, and direct Teachable checkout links

-   **Testimonials section** migrated from existing site

-   **About section** with accurate current business description

-   **Basic storefront page** showing available products with purchase
    links

-   **Contact/booking integration** linking to Monika's calendar

-   **Deployed and live** on Vercel or similar with a clean URL

**Team Allocation**

-   **Engineer 1--2:** Frontend build --- landing page, responsive
    design, Teachable integration

-   **Engineer 3:** Content migration --- extracting testimonials,
    product info, and imagery from existing site; writing copy

-   **Engineer 4:** Infrastructure --- deployment pipeline, domain
    configuration, analytics setup

**Checkpoint:** At hour 8, the landing page is live. A visitor can see
RPI Education's offerings, compare memberships, and click through to
purchase. The broken front door is fixed.

Phase 2: AI Advisor Core (Hours 8--18)
--------------------------------------

**Goal:** A working AI assistant trained on Monika's content that can
hold intelligent conversations about real estate investing and guide
visitors toward the right product.

**Deliverables**

-   **Content ingestion pipeline:** Process and embed all of Monika's
    training data (videos, PDFs, blog posts, podcast transcripts, course
    materials) into a vector database

-   **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) system:** AI assistant
    powered by a large language model with retrieval from Monika's
    content, ensuring responses are grounded in her actual frameworks
    and language

-   **Conversational UI:** Chat widget embedded in the landing page,
    available on every page, with a warm welcome message and suggested
    starter questions

-   **Prompt engineering:** Carefully crafted system prompts that
    capture Monika's tone (warm, empowering, action-oriented), enforce
    guardrails (no specific investment advice), and guide conversations
    toward product recommendations

-   **Product recommendation logic:** When the conversation reveals
    enough about the visitor's needs, the AI recommends a specific tier
    with a clear explanation and a purchase link

-   **Email capture:** After 2--3 exchanges, the AI prompts for an email
    address to "save the conversation and send personalized resources"

**Team Allocation**

-   **Engineer 1:** Content processing pipeline --- transcription,
    chunking, embedding

-   **Engineer 2:** RAG architecture --- vector store, retrieval logic,
    LLM integration

-   **Engineer 3:** Chat UI --- conversational interface, widget
    integration, mobile responsiveness

-   **Engineer 4:** Prompt engineering and testing --- writing system
    prompts, testing against real scenarios, refining tone

**Checkpoint:** At hour 18, visitors can have a real conversation with
"Digital Monika" about real estate investing and get intelligent,
personalized guidance. The AI can recommend the right membership tier
and link them to purchase.

Phase 3: Self-Serve Onboarding (Hours 18--24)
---------------------------------------------

**Goal:** A structured assessment flow that converts interested visitors
into paying members without a discovery call.

**Deliverables**

-   **Multi-step assessment wizard:** Beautiful, mobile-friendly flow
    with 4--6 questions about investing experience, goals, preferred
    style, and budget

-   **Smart recommendation engine:** Logic that maps assessment answers
    to the optimal product/tier, with a personalized explanation

-   **Results page:** A compelling, personalized results screen showing
    "Your recommended path" with the matched product, pricing, key
    benefits, and a prominent purchase button

-   **Email integration:** Assessment results emailed to the visitor and
    their data captured for CRM

-   **AI Advisor integration:** The assessment can be triggered from
    within an AI conversation, and results feed context back to the AI
    for continued guidance

**Team Allocation**

-   **Engineer 1--2:** Assessment UI and recommendation logic

-   **Engineer 3:** Backend --- data storage, email sending, Teachable
    deep linking

-   **Engineer 4:** AI integration --- connecting assessment data to the
    AI Advisor context

**Checkpoint:** At hour 24, visitors have two conversion paths: a guided
conversation with the AI Advisor, or a structured self-assessment. Both
end with a clear product recommendation and purchase link.

Phase 4: Lead Intelligence & CRM (Hours 24--30)
-----------------------------------------------

**Goal:** Every visitor interaction feeds intelligence back into Keap,
making the newsletter and follow-up process dramatically smarter.

**Deliverables**

-   **Keap API integration:** New contacts created automatically;

-   **Auto-tagging:** Contacts tagged based on interests, experience
    level, and intent signals from AI conversations and assessments

-   **Lead scoring:** Automated score based on engagement depth (pages
    visited, conversation length, assessment completion, return visits)

-   **Hot lead notifications:** Email or SMS alert to Monika when a
    high-score lead is identified

-   **Basic admin dashboard:** Simple web view showing visitor count,
    conversations started, assessments completed, leads generated, and
    top questions asked

**Checkpoint:** At hour 30, the full system is operational. Visitors
land on a modern site, engage with the AI Advisor or assessment, and
their data flows into Keap with tags and scores. Monika gets alerts for
hot leads. The 30-hour weekend is complete.

**Goal:** Turn Monika's existing content into a search engine magnet

Phase 7: Member Portal & Retention (Hours 75--100)

-   **Referral system:** Member referral tracking with incentives (e.g.,
    refer a friend, get a month free)

-   **Revenue analytics:** Dashboard showing conversion rates by
    channel, product revenue, member lifetime value, and churn

5.2 Key Integrations
--------------------

**Teachable Integration**

Teachable is the existing course platform hosting the Wealth Immersion
program and potentially other products. Integration points:

-   **Checkout deep links:** Direct URLs to specific product/tier
    purchase pages. Teachable supports custom checkout URLs with coupon
    pre-population.

-   **Webhook notifications:** Teachable can fire webhooks on
    enrollment, which can trigger CRM updates and welcome emails.

-   **SSO (Phase 7):** Teachable supports SSO on higher-tier plans.
    Confirm Monika's Teachable plan level.

**Keap (Infusionsoft) Integration**

Keap is the existing CRM with 2,000 contacts. Integration points:

-   **Contact API:** Create new contacts, update existing contacts, add
    custom fields for AI interaction data.

-   **Tag API:** Apply tags for segmentation (interest areas, intent
    level, product recommendations).

-   **Campaign triggers:** Keap campaigns can be triggered by API
    actions, enabling automated follow-up sequences.

-   **Rate limits:** Keap API has rate limits (varies by plan). Batch
    operations and implement queuing if needed.

5.4 Security & Compliance Considerations
----------------------------------------

-   **Financial advice disclaimer:** The AI must clearly state it
    provides education, not licensed financial advice. This should be
    visible in the UI and included in every AI conversation.

-   **Data privacy:** Visitor data collection requires a privacy policy.
    PIPEDA compliance for Canadian operations; consider GDPR for
    Australian/European members.

-   **Payment handling:** All payments should go through Teachable's
    payment processor (Stripe). RPI Helper should never handle credit
    card data directly.

-   **Content accuracy:** The RAG system must be configured to decline
    answering questions it can't confidently answer from the training
    data, rather than hallucinating.

8. Guidance for the Engineering Team
====================================

  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  *This is Top Gun, and Monika is directing the pilots. You have 30 hours to build something that will change a business. Here's how to think about it.*
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8.1 Mindset
-----------

You are not building a hackathon demo. You are building real software
that a real business owner will use starting Monday morning. Every
decision should optimize for "will this actually work in production?"
not "will this look impressive in a presentation?"

Monika has spent years building her expertise and reputation. The AI
Advisor carries her name and her brand. Treat it with the same care she
would. If the AI gives bad advice, it's Monika's reputation on the line.
Quality over quantity.

8.2 Priorities
--------------

If you're ever unsure what to work on next, follow this priority stack:

-   **Priority 1: Does the landing page work and look professional?**
    This is the minimum viable product. Everything else is useless
    without a working front door.

-   **Priority 2: Can the AI Advisor hold an intelligent conversation?**
    This is the differentiator. A mediocre chatbot is worse than no
    chatbot. Invest in prompt engineering and content quality.

-   **Priority 3: Can a visitor self-serve to purchase?** The whole
    point is conversion. If someone is ready to buy, the path must be
    frictionless.

-   **Priority 4: Is visitor data being captured?** Even if CRM
    integration isn't complete, store everything in the database.
    Integration can happen later.

8.3 What Monika Will Provide
----------------------------

-   **A shared folder with all training content** (videos, transcripts,
    PDFs, course materials) organized and labeled

-   **Teachable access** with product URLs and checkout links

-   **Keap API credentials** with documentation on existing tags and
    segments

-   **Brand assets** including logo, headshot, colors, and any existing
    guidelines

-   **Availability during the hackathon** for questions, content review,
    and AI tone validation

-   **The 5 most common discovery call questions** with how she
    typically answers them

8.4 What Good Looks Like
------------------------

At the end of the hackathon weekend, imagine Monika shares the new site
on LinkedIn with her audience. A prospect who heard her on a podcast
last week clicks the link. They land on a clean, modern page. They see
the membership tiers and think "interesting, but I have questions." They
click the chat widget. The AI greets them warmly and asks what they're
looking to achieve. They say they want to start investing in rental
properties but don't know where to begin. The AI explains RPI's
approach, asks a few questions about their situation, and recommends the
Wealth Immersion program. It explains why, shares what they'll learn,
and provides a direct link to enroll. They click, purchase, and become
member \#101.

That is what good looks like. Build that.
